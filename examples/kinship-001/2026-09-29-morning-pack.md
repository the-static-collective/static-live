### Goose Day

**Claim mode:** observed + folklore clearly labeled

September 29 is also Goose Day in Pennsylvania's Juniata River Valley. A William G. Pomeroy Foundation historic marker describes it as a regional celebration rooted in Michaelmas; the local legend says eating goose on September 29 brings prosperity for the coming year. The historical tradition also connects Michaelmas with settling rents, sometimes involving a goose.

Source:
- https://www.wgpfoundation.org/historic-markers/goose-day/

**Accuracy note:** call this a **regional Pennsylvania Goose Day**, not a national holiday.

**Compression worth keeping:** “settle the rent, bring a goose, then eat the goose.”

That is a comic compression of the tradition, not a literal description of every historical tenancy arrangement.
