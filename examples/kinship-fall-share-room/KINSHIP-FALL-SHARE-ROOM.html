STATION MEMORY · TOASTER ANCESTRY
The station remembers without deciding

          
Memory begins only after a human confirms something actually aired.

        


        

          Memory may change what the producer is inclined to try and what the room is inclined to notice. It may not change what actually happened.
          Past air ≠ current truth. Human verdict ≠ audience preference model. Re-open ≠ replay.
        


        
Memory pressures

        


        
Past Airs

        
KEEP means “worth carrying forward.” WEIRD preserves a strange encounter without deciding whether it was good. COMPOST means “let this feed history without pushing repetition.” A later verdict appends; it does not erase the earlier one.
